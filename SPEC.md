## Product boundary

The product is the same white T-shirt throughout the comparison. The baseline offers a size selector and temporary demo bag. No real orders, payment, accounts, or persistence. Keep product facts stable between directions; do not add unsupported claims about materials, manufacturing, sustainability, fit, delivery, scarcity, reviews, or sales.

The learner's intended point of difference is a white tee for wearer-created art, potentially using paint, especially for the Creator character. This is a positioning hypothesis, not a current product feature or claim; materials, supplies, care, paint suitability, and comparative advantage are not established.

The learner describes the intended shirt as relaxed in fit with a boxy-looking silhouette, and suggests a size chart to help visitors choose. This is a design description; the exact garment, measurements, and chart are not identified or verified.

The learner wants the storefront to start with the shirt shown by itself and to offer clickable additional views, specifically a side view, a back view, and the shirt worn by a model. If available, they prefer a model image featuring a 6'3" Black man. The learner will provide authorized photos/assets; do not invent angles, fit photography, measurements, or garment details while those assets are pending.

The learner provided Prada's U.S. [Short-sleeved linen T-shirt page](https://www.prada.com/us/en/p/short-sleeved-linen-t-shirt/P24D3Q_193R_F0009_S_OOO) and its [direct product image](https://www.prada.com/content/dam/pradabkg_products/P/P24/P24D3Q/193RF0009/P24D3Q_193R_F0009_S_OOO_MDD.jpg/_jcr_content/renditions/cq5dam.web.hebebed.2400.2400.jpg) as product-display references because they do not have a physical shirt. The page's image was initially blocked by a cookie notice; the direct image later showed a model wearing a white shirt against a light, neutral background. Use only broad presentation cues such as making the shirt easy to see. Do not copy Prada imagery or branding, and do not transfer its product details or price to the learner's shirt.

The learner also shared a [DekGrativ-hosted “Boxy Fit Tees Mockup” image](https://dekgrativ.com/cdn/shop/files/web_free_boxy_fit_2025_-_dekgrafis_slide_1_c6a4b67b-fee1-473e-974d-15e3013fbe60.webp?v=1747389061) and reports the source permits mockup use. The learner then supplied [Artboard_2.webp](public/assets/white-tee-sample-art.webp), reported its mockup use is permitted, and approved its white front/back views for the storefront gallery. The learner later clarified the desired side, back, and on-model views; these are not present in the supplied images. The artwork printed on the current concept mockup is sample artwork, not part of the blank shirt being sold, and does not verify the exact blank or fit.

The learner requested a black notification with white text after adding a selected size, including a direct way to reach the bag. It normally disappears after four seconds (remaining visible while keyboard focus is inside it). The persistent header “Demo bag” link is styled like a button and opens the bag, where each white T-shirt, selected size, quantity, unit price, and fictional demo total are shown.
