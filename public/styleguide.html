Direction selected / identity in progress

Audience

Creative people

Primary hypothesis: creative visitors who want to express an individual point of view. Contrast check: children may want a fun activity or personalized gift. Neither is validated.

Archetype

Creator

The brand character, not a label for every customer.

Visual direction

Postmodernist-leaning

Layered colors signal individualism; keep the T-shirt as the focus. This is a design prediction, not audience evidence.

The learner predicts that an original, carefully aligned presentation may stand apart from generic AI or streetwear branding. The art should support the T-shirt, not overshadow it; this effect still needs audience feedback.
